Good to know

Questions sellers often ask.

Every property is different. These answers are a starting point, not a substitute for a property-specific discussion.

What types of commercial property do you buy?

The business says it considers a range of UK commercial properties, including shops, offices, industrial units, pubs, restaurants and other premises. Eligibility depends on the individual property.

How quickly can I receive an offer?

The company's existing proposition advertises initial cash offers within 24 hours after property details are submitted. This is an initial offer timeframe, not a guarantee of completion.

Do I pay estate-agent fees?

The business states that it buys directly and does not charge estate-agent selling fees. Ask for the proposed terms and any other costs to be confirmed in writing.

Will you consider vacant property?

The company says it considers vacant commercial property. Share the location, current condition and any relevant information for an initial review.

How do I start?

Use the enquiry button below or contact the team directly. Property address, type, ownership status and rental details can help the team assess the opportunity.
